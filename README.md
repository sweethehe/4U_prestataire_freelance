# 4U_prestataire_freelance

4U Partner (Provider Application)

4U Partner is a mobile application dedicated to service providers who wish to offer their services on the 4U platform. It allows drivers, delivery personnel, dry cleaners, mechanics, and maintenance agents to receive, track, and manage user orders in real-time.

It aims to provide a simple and effective tool to connect professionals with clients, optimize their business activities, and facilitate the daily management of their services.
